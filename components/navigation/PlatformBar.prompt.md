One-sentence: The deep-navy platform rail that sits above the whole app shell, naming the platform that owns the current module.

```jsx
<AppShell
  platformBar={<PlatformBar eyebrow="TDH AI" title="TDH AI Platform" />}
  product="Permitting & Reg"
  …
/>
```

Navy `--brand-navy` (#1C3A5C) appears **here and nowhere else** — it separates platform chrome from the teal product system. `eyebrow` defaults to "TDH AI"; `right` takes an optional account or environment tag.
